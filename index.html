DustBank

Iterative Infinity

Sell the dust tokens in your Algorand wallet for ALGO. Each token is closed out too, which frees the 0.1 ALGO it was locking. You sign everything in your own wallet; nobody else ever holds your coins.

The fee is 1%. A contract on chain checks every sale and refuses a bigger fee, or any sale that leaves you behind. Holders of Iterative Infinity pay nothing.

TestNet: its ALGO has no value. MainNet: not deployed yet.
